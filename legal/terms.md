## 2. Technical Diving Safety and Non-Medical Disclaimer

* **Mathematical Modeling Only:** This software computes theoretical decompression schedules, oxygen toxicity indices, and gas-blending tables using published mathematical models: Bühlmann ZH-L16C (`1b` and `1`), Haldane and Schreiner inert gas transport equations, Erik C. Baker Gradient Factors, NOAA CNS %, Lambertsen/Baker OTU, and Real-Gas Virial Z-factor equations.
* **No Life-Support Certification:** This software is not a certified dive computer, medical device, or life-support system. Decompression sickness (DCS), central nervous system or pulmonary oxygen toxicity, hypoxia, hypercapnia, and gas-blending errors can cause severe injury or death.
* **Diver Responsibility:** Users must hold appropriate scuba diving and gas-blending training, independently verify all dive plans and cylinder gas compositions (including physical oxygen and helium gas analysis prior to diving), and follow recognized diving agency procedures.
